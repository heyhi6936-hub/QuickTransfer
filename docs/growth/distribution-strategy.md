## 1. High-Value Link Earning Opportunities

Avoid low-quality directory submission, PBNs, or comment spam. Instead, focus on legitimate editorial citations:

1. **Free Utility Curations:**
   - Free tool roundups (e.g. "Best Free Tools to Send Files Without Creating an Account").
   - Student and university productivity resource guides.
   - Remote work and digital nomad toolkits.
2. **Productivity & Tech Forums:**
   - Problem-solving answers on Reddit (r/productivity, r/freesoftware, r/techsupport) answering specific questions such as "How do I move photos from iPhone to Windows PC without iTunes?".
   - Developer & open-source community showcases.
3. **Software Directories with Editorial Standards:**
   - AlternativeTo (listed under WeTransfer and AirDrop alternatives).
   - Product Hunt launch for QuickTransfer 2.0.
   - Slant.co and SaaSHub.

---

## 2. Product-Led Distribution Loops

1. **Self-Propelled Recipient Loop:**
   - Every sender introduces at least one recipient to QuickTransfer.
   - On the `ResultView` screen for recipients, include a subtle CTA: *"Want to send a file back? QuickTransfer is 100% free and requires no sign-up."*
2. **Shareable QR Codes:**
   - Instant QR code rendering on the sender's screen makes device-to-device transfers effortless in physical proximity (e.g. meeting rooms, classrooms, co-working spaces).
3. **Transparent Privacy Comparison:**
   - Maintain objective, dated comparison tables against traditional email attachment limits (25MB) and account-gated cloud drives, highlighting speed and temporary privacy.
